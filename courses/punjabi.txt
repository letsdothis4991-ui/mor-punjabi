UNIT Gurmukhi letters | The consonants of the Gurmukhi script
TIP Gurmukhi is written left to right, and most letters hang from a line along the top.
TIP Every consonant carries a built-in "a" sound, so ਕ on its own is "ka".
TIP Each letter's name doubles its sound: ਕ is called kakkā and ਸ is sassā.

UNIT Vowels and marks | Vowel signs, vowel letters and special marks
TIP Vowel signs (lagāṃ mātrāṃ) attach to a consonant: ਕ + ਾ = ਕਾ (kā).
TIP ਿ (sihārī) is written before the letter but said after it: ਕਿ is "ki".
TIP At the start of a word a vowel sits on a carrier letter, ੳ, ਅ or ੲ: ਅ + ਾ = ਆ (ā).
TIP ੱ (addak) doubles the next consonant, and ਂ (bindī) or ੰ (ṭippī) add a nasal sound.

UNIT Hello | Greetings, politeness and introducing yourself
TIP ਸਤ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ (sat srī akāl) works for both hello and goodbye.
TIP Use ਤੁਸੀਂ (tusīṃ) for anyone older or anyone you want to respect. Keep ਤੂੰ (tūṃ) for close friends and children.
TIP Add ਜੀ (jī) to be polite: ਹਾਂ ਜੀ (yes), ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ (no), ਸਿਮਰਨ ਜੀ.
TIP "To be": ਮੈਂ ਹਾਂ (I am), ਤੁਸੀਂ ਹੋ (you are), ਉਹ ਹੈ (he or she is), ਅਸੀਂ ਹਾਂ (we are), ਉਹ ਹਨ (they are).

TIP Words like "in" and "on" come after the noun: ਘਰ ਵਿੱਚ (in the house), ਮੇਜ਼ ਉੱਤੇ (on the table).

UNIT Have done | Say what has happened and what is already finished
TIP Add ਹੈ to the past to say "has done": ਉਹ ਆਇਆ (he came), ਉਹ ਆਇਆ ਹੈ (he has come).
TIP ਚੁੱਕਾ means "already": ਮੈਂ ਖਾ ਚੁੱਕਾ ਹਾਂ (I have already eaten, said by a man), ਮੈਂ ਖਾ ਚੁੱਕੀ ਹਾਂ (said by a woman).
TIP Helper verbs add flavour: ਲੈਣਾ when you do it for yourself (ਖਾ ਲਿਆ, ate it up), ਦੇਣਾ when it's for someone else (ਦੱਸ ਦਿੱਤਾ, told them), ਜਾਣਾ for a change of state (ਸੌਂ ਗਿਆ, fell asleep).

TIP ਜੀ makes anything polite: ਹਾਂ ਜੀ (yes), ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ (no), ਆਓ ਜੀ (come in).
TIP ਬਹੁਤ ਸੁਆਦ ਆਇਆ (I really enjoyed it) is the best thanks a cook can get.

X ਇੱਕ ਮਿੱਠੀ ਲੱਸੀ ਵੀ ਲੈ ਆਓ। | ikk miṭṭhī lassī vī lai āo. | Bring a sweet lassi too. | That's for the dhaba!
H ਜਿਉਂਦੇ ਵੱਸਦੇ ਰਹੋ! | jiunde vassde raho! | Live long and be happy!

SCENE bazaar | Haggling in the bazaar | ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਭਾਅ | The shopkeeper | You want a phulkari dupatta, at a fair price
TIP ਭਾਅ (bhā) is the going price, and ਭਾਅ ਕਰਨਾ is to haggle. In a bazaar the first price is only the start.
TIP ਬੋਹਣੀ (bohṇī) is a shop's first sale of the day. It's thought to bring luck, so shopkeepers often give the first customer a good deal.
TIP Hundreds are counted in hundreds: ਬਾਰਾਂ ਸੌ (twelve hundred) is 1200 and ਪੰਦਰਾਂ ਸੌ is 1500.
H ਆਓ ਜੀ, ਕੀ ਦਿਖਾਵਾਂ? | āo jī, kī dikhāvāṃ? | Come in, what can I show you?
R ਫੁਲਕਾਰੀ ਵਾਲੀ ਚੁੰਨੀ ਦਿਖਾਓ। | phulkārī vālī chunnī dikhāo. | Show me a phulkari dupatta.
X ਮੈਨੂੰ ਭੁੱਖ ਲੱਗੀ ਹੈ। | mainūṃ bhukkh laggī hai. | I'm hungry. | This is a cloth shop, not a dhaba!
X ਪਿੰਡ ਕਦੋਂ ਆਉਣਾ ਹੈ? | piṇḍ kadoṃ āuṇā hai? | When are you coming to the village? | That's Nani's question! Tell him what you want to see.
H ਇਹ ਦੇਖੋ, ਹੱਥ ਦੀ ਕਢਾਈ ਹੈ। ਬਹੁਤ ਸੋਹਣੀ ਹੈ। | ih dekho, hatth dī kaḍhāī hai. bahut sohṇī hai. | Look at this, it's embroidered by hand. It's very beautiful.
R ਸੋਹਣੀ ਤਾਂ ਹੈ। ਕਿੰਨੇ ਦੀ ਹੈ? | sohṇī tāṃ hai. kinne dī hai? | It is pretty. How much is it?
X ਇਹ ਕਿਸ ਦਾ ਘਰ ਹੈ? | ih kis dā ghar hai? | Whose house is this? | That asks whose house this is. Ask the price.
X ਮੈਂ ਨਹੀਂ ਖਾਣੀ। | maiṃ nahīṃ khāṇī. | I won't eat it. | You don't eat a dupatta!
H ਤੁਹਾਡੇ ਲਈ ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਦੋ ਹਜ਼ਾਰ ਰੁਪਏ। | tuhāḍe laī siraf do hazār rupae. | For you, only two thousand rupees.
R ਦੋ ਹਜ਼ਾਰ? ਬਹੁਤ ਮਹਿੰਗੀ ਹੈ! ਬਾਰਾਂ ਸੌ ਲਾ ਲਓ। | do hazār? bahut mahiṅgī hai! bārāṃ sau lā lao. | Two thousand? That's too expensive! Make it twelve hundred.
X ਦੋ ਹਜ਼ਾਰ? ਬਹੁਤ ਸਸਤੀ ਹੈ! ਤਿੰਨ ਹਜ਼ਾਰ ਲੈ ਲਓ। | do hazār? bahut sastī hai! tinn hazār lai lao. | Two thousand? That's cheap! Take three thousand. | That says it's cheap and offers more. Not how haggling works!
X ਦੋ ਹਜ਼ਾਰ? ਠੀਕ ਹੈ, ਛੇਤੀ ਪੈਕ ਕਰੋ। | do hazār? ṭhīk hai, chhetī paik karo. | Two thousand? Fine, pack it quickly. | You could, but you came to haggle. Start lower!
H ਨਹੀਂ ਜੀ, ਏਨੇ ਦੀ ਤਾਂ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਆਪ ਨਹੀਂ ਪੈਂਦੀ। ਸਤਾਰਾਂ ਸੌ ਦੇ ਦਿਓ। | nahīṃ jī, ene dī tāṃ mainūṃ āp nahīṃ paindī. satārāṃ sau de dio. | No, it costs me more than that myself. Give me seventeen hundred.
R ਪੰਦਰਾਂ ਸੌ, ਬੱਸ। ਨਹੀਂ ਤਾਂ ਅਗਲੀ ਦੁਕਾਨ ਦੇਖਦੇ ਹਾਂ। | pandrāṃ sau, bas. nahīṃ tāṃ aglī dukān dekhde hāṃ. | Fifteen hundred, that's it. Otherwise we'll try the next shop.
X ਪੱਚੀ ਸੌ, ਬੱਸ। | pacchī sau, bas. | Twenty-five hundred, that's it. | That's more than he asked for!
X ਸਤ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਅਕਾਲ ਜੀ! | sat srī akāl jī! | Hello! | That's hello. You're in the middle of a deal!
H ਚਲੋ ਠੀਕ ਹੈ, ਤੁਹਾਡੇ ਲਈ ਪੰਦਰਾਂ ਸੌ। ਬੋਹਣੀ ਦਾ ਟਾਈਮ ਹੈ। | chalo ṭhīk hai, tuhāḍe laī pandrāṃ sau. bohṇī dā ṭāīm hai. | All right, fifteen hundred for you. It's my first sale of the day.
R ਧੰਨਵਾਦ ਜੀ! ਇੱਕ ਲਿਫ਼ਾਫ਼ਾ ਵੀ ਦੇ ਦਿਓ। | dhannvād jī! ikk lifāfā vī de dio. | Thank you! Give me a bag too, please.
X ਨਹੀਂ, ਹੁਣ ਮੈਨੂੰ ਨਹੀਂ ਚਾਹੀਦੀ। | nahīṃ, huṇ mainūṃ nahīṃ chāhīdī. | No, I don't want it now. | He agreed to your price! Backing out now would be rude.
X ਜੁਗ ਜੁਗ ਜੀਓ! | jug jug jīo! | Bless you! | That's a blessing elders give. Thank him instead.

SCENE auto | An auto to the station | ਆਟੋ ਵਿੱਚ | The auto driver | Your train leaves at ten and you're running late
TIP ਗੱਡੀ means a train and also a car. ਮੇਰੀ ਗੱਡੀ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਹੈ can only mean a train, because a car doesn't leave at ten.
TIP Punjabi has words for fractions of a hundred: ਸਵਾ ਸੌ (125), ਡੇਢ ਸੌ (150), ਢਾਈ ਸੌ (250).
TIP Agree the fare before you get in. ___ ਚੱਲੋਗੇ? (will you go to ___?) is the way to ask.
CUE You wave down an auto rickshaw.
R ਭਾਜੀ, ਰੇਲਵੇ ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਚੱਲੋਗੇ? | bhājī, relve saṭeshan challoge? | Bhaji, will you go to the railway station?
X ਭਾਜੀ, ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨਾਮ ਕੀ ਹੈ? | bhājī, tuhāḍā nām kī hai? | Bhaji, what's your name? | Ask if he'll take you to the station first.
X ਭਾਜੀ, ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਬਹੁਤ ਸੋਹਣਾ ਹੈ। | bhājī, saṭeshan bahut sohṇā hai. | Bhaji, the station is very beautiful. | That says the station is pretty. Ask him to take you there.
H ਬੈਠੋ ਜੀ। ਡੇਢ ਸੌ ਰੁਪਏ ਲੱਗਣਗੇ। | baiṭho jī. ḍeḍh sau rupae laggaṇge. | Get in. It'll be a hundred and fifty rupees.
R ਸੌ ਰੁਪਏ ਠੀਕ ਹਨ, ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਨੇੜੇ ਹੀ ਹੈ। | sau rupae ṭhīk han, saṭeshan neṛe hī hai. | A hundred is fair, the station is close by.
X ਡੇਢ ਸੌ? ਦੋ ਸੌ ਲੈ ਲਓ। | ḍeḍh sau? do sau lai lao. | A hundred and fifty? Take two hundred. | You're offering him more!
X ਮੈਨੂੰ ਨਹੀਂ ਪਤਾ। | mainūṃ nahīṃ patā. | I don't know. | Offer a price.
H ਚਲੋ, ਸਵਾ ਸੌ ਦੇ ਦਿਓ। ਬੈਠੋ। | chalo, savā sau de dio. baiṭho. | All right, give me a hundred and twenty-five. Get in.
R ਠੀਕ ਹੈ। ਥੋੜ੍ਹਾ ਜਲਦੀ ਚੱਲਿਓ, ਮੇਰੀ ਗੱਡੀ ਦਸ ਵਜੇ ਹੈ। | ṭhīk hai. thoṛhā jaldī challio, merī gaḍḍī das vaje hai. | Okay. Please go a bit fast, my train is at ten.
X ਠੀਕ ਹੈ। ਹੌਲੀ ਹੌਲੀ ਚੱਲਿਓ, ਬਹੁਤ ਟਾਈਮ ਹੈ। | ṭhīk hai. haulī haulī challio, bahut ṭāīm hai. | Okay. Go slowly, there's lots of time. | You're running late! Ask him to hurry.
X ਮੇਰੀ ਗੱਡੀ ਲਾਲ ਹੈ। | merī gaḍḍī lāl hai. | My car is red. | That's about the colour of your car. Tell him when your train is.
H ਫ਼ਿਕਰ ਨਾ ਕਰੋ, ਦਸ ਮਿੰਟਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਪਹੁੰਚਾ ਦਿਆਂਗੇ। | fikar nā karo, das miṇṭāṃ vich pahunchā diāṅge. | Don't worry, we'll get you there in ten minutes.
CUE The station is right ahead.
R ਬੱਸ ਇੱਥੇ ਹੀ ਰੋਕ ਦਿਓ। ਇਹ ਲਓ ਪੈਸੇ। | bas itthe hī rok dio. ih lao paise. | Stop right here. Here's the money.
X ਸਿੱਧੇ ਚੱਲੋ, ਹਾਲੇ ਦੂਰ ਹੈ। | siddhe challo, hāle dūr hai. | Keep going, it's still far. | You've arrived! Ask him to stop.
X ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਕੀ ਹਾਲ ਹੈ? | tuhāḍā kī hāl hai? | How are you? | No time to chat, you've got a train to catch!
H ਸ਼ੁਕਰੀਆ ਜੀ। ਸਫ਼ਰ ਵਧੀਆ ਰਹੇ! | shukrīā jī. safar vadhīā rahe! | Thank you. Have a good journey!

SCENE bhua | Visiting Bhua | ਭੂਆ ਦੇ ਘਰ | Bhua ji | You visit your aunt, your dad's sister
TIP ਭੂਆ is your dad's sister. Your mum's sister is ਮਾਸੀ.
TIP Bring something sweet (ਮਠਿਆਈ) when you visit. Your host will say ਇਹਦੀ ਕੀ ਲੋੜ ਸੀ? (there was no need!), but they're pleased.
TIP A Punjabi host always offers more food. Saying no once is not enough, so ਢਿੱਡ ਭਰ ਗਿਆ (I'm full) is a phrase worth knowing!
CUE You ring the bell. Bhua opens the door with a big smile.
H ਆਓ ਆਓ, ਲੰਘ ਆਓ! ਰਸਤੇ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੋਈ ਤਕਲੀਫ਼ ਤਾਂ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੋਈ? | āo āo, laṅgh āo! raste vich koī takalīf tāṃ nahīṃ hoī? | Come in, come in! Any trouble on the way?
R ਨਹੀਂ ਭੂਆ ਜੀ, ਸਭ ਠੀਕ ਸੀ। ਇਹ ਮਠਿਆਈ ਤੁਹਾਡੇ ਲਈ। | nahīṃ bhūā jī, sabh ṭhīk sī. ih maṭhiāī tuhāḍe laī. | No Bhua ji, it was all fine. These sweets are for you.
X ਨਹੀਂ ਭੂਆ ਜੀ, ਮੈਂ ਅੰਦਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਉਣਾ। | nahīṃ bhūā jī, maiṃ andar nahīṃ āuṇā. | No Bhua ji, I'm not coming in. | That says you won't come in!
X ਬਹੁਤ ਮਹਿੰਗੀ ਹੈ! | bahut mahiṅgī hai! | It's too expensive! | That's for haggling in the bazaar.
H ਇਹਦੀ ਕੀ ਲੋੜ ਸੀ? ਬੈਠੋ, ਚਾਹ ਪੀਓਗੇ ਜਾਂ ਲੱਸੀ? | ihdī kī loṛ sī? baiṭho, chāh pīoge jāṃ lassī? | There was no need for this! Sit down. Will you have tea or lassi?
R ਚਾਹ ਠੀਕ ਹੈ, ਪਰ ਖੰਡ ਘੱਟ ਪਾਇਓ। | chāh ṭhīk hai, par khaṇḍ ghaṭṭ pāio. | Tea is good, but go easy on the sugar.
X ਚਾਹ ਕਿੰਨੇ ਦੀ ਹੈ? | chāh kinne dī hai? | How much is the tea? | You don't pay at your aunt's house!
X ਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮੀਂਹ ਪਿਆ ਸੀ। | kallh mīṃh piā sī. | It rained yesterday. | She asked tea or lassi.
CUE Tea comes, then pakoras, then hot parathas.
H ਲੈ, ਇੱਕ ਪਰੌਂਠਾ ਹੋਰ ਲੈ। ਤੂੰ ਤਾਂ ਕੁਝ ਖਾਧਾ ਹੀ ਨਹੀਂ! | lai, ikk parauṇṭhā hor lai. tūṃ tāṃ kujh khādhā hī nahīṃ! | Here, have another paratha. You've hardly eaten anything!
R ਨਹੀਂ ਭੂਆ ਜੀ, ਬੱਸ। ਢਿੱਡ ਭਰ ਗਿਆ। | nahīṃ bhūā jī, bas. ḍhiḍḍ bhar giā. | No Bhua ji, that's enough. I'm full.
X ਹਾਂ ਜੀ, ਪੰਜ ਹੋਰ ਲਿਆਓ! | hāṃ jī, panj hor liāo! | Yes, bring five more! | Bhua would love that, but you're full. Say no politely.
X ਪਰੌਂਠਾ ਠੰਢਾ ਹੈ। | parauṇṭhā ṭhaṇḍhā hai. | The paratha is cold. | That's a complaint. Not kind to your aunt!
H ਐਵੇਂ ਨਾ ਕਰ! ਅੱਧਾ ਤਾਂ ਲੈ ਲੈ। | aiveṃ nā kar! addhā tāṃ lai lai. | Don't be silly! At least have half.
R ਚੰਗਾ, ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਅੱਧਾ। ਤੁਹਾਡੇ ਹੱਥ ਦਾ ਖਾਣਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਸੁਆਦ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹੈ! | changā, siraf addhā. tuhāḍe hatth dā khāṇā bahut suād hundā hai! | Okay, just half. Your cooking is always so tasty!
X ਨਹੀਂ, ਮੈਂ ਘਰ ਜਾ ਕੇ ਖਾਣਾ ਹੈ। | nahīṃ, maiṃ ghar jā ke khāṇā hai. | No, I'll eat when I get home. | Bhua would be hurt! Take the half.
X ਪਿਛਲੇ ਸਾਲ, ਭੂਆ ਜੀ। | pichhle sāl, bhūā jī. | Last year, Bhua. | That doesn't answer her.
H ਵਾਹ! ਹੁਣ ਅੱਜ ਰਾਤ ਇੱਥੇ ਹੀ ਰਹੋ। | vāh! huṇ ajj rāt itthe hī raho. | Wonderful! Now stay here tonight.

SCENE doctor | At the doctor's | ਡਾਕਟਰ ਕੋਲ | The doctor | You've felt unwell for a couple of days
TIP ਡਾਕਟਰ ਸਾਹਿਬ is the polite way to speak to a doctor. ਸਾਹਿਬ shows respect.
TIP ___ ਤੋਂ means "since" or "for" with time: ਦੋ ਦਿਨਾਂ ਤੋਂ ਬੁਖ਼ਾਰ ਹੈ (I've had a fever for two days).
TIP Listen for the number: ਤਿੰਨ (three) and ਤੀਹ (thirty) sound close.
H ਆਓ, ਬੈਠੋ। ਦੱਸੋ, ਕੀ ਤਕਲੀਫ਼ ਹੈ? | āo, baiṭho. dasso, kī takalīf hai? | Come, sit. Tell me, what's the trouble?
R ਡਾਕਟਰ ਸਾਹਿਬ, ਦੋ ਦਿਨਾਂ ਤੋਂ ਬੁਖ਼ਾਰ ਹੈ ਤੇ ਗਲਾ ਖ਼ਰਾਬ ਹੈ। | ḍākṭar sāhib, do dināṃ toṃ bukhār hai te galā kharāb hai. | Doctor, I've had a fever for two days and a sore throat.
X ਡਾਕਟਰ ਸਾਹਿਬ, ਮੈਂ ਬਿਲਕੁਲ ਠੀਕ ਹਾਂ। | ḍākṭar sāhib, maiṃ bilkul ṭhīk hāṃ. | Doctor, I'm perfectly well. | Then why are you here? Tell the doctor what's wrong.
X ਡਾਕਟਰ ਸਾਹਿਬ, ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਚੱਲੋਗੇ? | ḍākṭar sāhib, saṭeshan challoge? | Doctor, will you go to the station? | That's for the auto driver!
H ਅੱਛਾ, ਮੂੰਹ ਖੋਲ੍ਹੋ। ਖੰਘ ਵੀ ਹੈ? | acchā, mūṃh kholho. khaṅgh vī hai? | I see, open your mouth. Do you have a cough too?
R ਹਾਂ ਜੀ, ਰਾਤ ਨੂੰ ਖੰਘ ਬਹੁਤ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ। | hāṃ jī, rāt nūṃ khaṅgh bahut āundī hai. | Yes, I cough a lot at night.
X ਨਹੀਂ, ਮੇਰਾ ਮੂੰਹ ਬੰਦ ਹੈ। | nahīṃ, merā mūṃh band hai. | No, my mouth is closed. | That says your mouth is shut! Answer about the cough.
X ਹਾਂ ਜੀ, ਢਿੱਡ ਭਰ ਗਿਆ। | hāṃ jī, ḍhiḍḍ bhar giā. | Yes, I'm full. | That's what you say at Bhua's table.
H ਕੋਈ ਗੱਲ ਨਹੀਂ, ਵਾਇਰਲ ਹੈ। ਇਹ ਦਵਾਈ ਦਿਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਤਿੰਨ ਵਾਰ ਖਾਣੀ ਹੈ। | koī gall nahīṃ, vāiral hai. ih davāī din vich tinn vār khāṇī hai. | Nothing serious, it's viral. Take this medicine three times a day.
R ਖਾਣੇ ਤੋਂ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ ਜਾਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਵਿੱਚ? | khāṇe toṃ pahilāṃ jāṃ bāad vich? | Before or after food?
X ਦਿਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਤੀਹ ਵਾਰ? | din vich tīh vār? | Thirty times a day? | Listen again: he said ਤਿੰਨ (three), not ਤੀਹ (thirty).
X ਦਵਾਈ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਹਿੰਗੀ ਹੈ! ਅੱਧੇ ਲਾ ਲਓ। | davāī bahut mahiṅgī hai! addhe lā lao. | The medicine is too expensive! Charge half. | No haggling with the doctor!
H ਖਾਣੇ ਤੋਂ ਬਾਅਦ। ਗਰਮ ਪਾਣੀ ਪੀਓ ਤੇ ਆਰਾਮ ਕਰੋ। | khāṇe toṃ bāad. garam pāṇī pīo te ārām karo. | After food. Drink warm water and rest.
R ਠੀਕ ਹੈ, ਬਹੁਤ ਬਹੁਤ ਧੰਨਵਾਦ ਡਾਕਟਰ ਸਾਹਿਬ। | ṭhīk hai, bahut bahut dhannvād ḍākṭar sāhib. | Okay, thank you very much, doctor.
X ਠੀਕ ਹੈ, ਅੱਜ ਰਾਤ ਨੱਚਣ ਚੱਲਾਂਗੇ! | ṭhīk hai, ajj rāt nacchaṇ challāṅge! | Okay, we'll go dancing tonight! | He said rest. No bhangra tonight!
X ਧੰਨਵਾਦ ਜੀ, ਫੇਰ ਆਇਓ! | dhannvād jī, pher āio! | Thanks, come again! | That's what a host says to a guest. Just thank the doctor.
H ਛੇਤੀ ਠੀਕ ਹੋ ਜਾਓਗੇ। ਤਿੰਨ ਦਿਨਾਂ ਬਾਅਦ ਫੇਰ ਦਿਖਾ ਜਾਣਾ। | chhetī ṭhīk ho jāoge. tinn dināṃ bāad pher dikhā jāṇā. | You'll be better soon. Come back for a check in three days.
